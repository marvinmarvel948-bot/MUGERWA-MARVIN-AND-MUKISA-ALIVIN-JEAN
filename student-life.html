Student Life

Beyond the classroom — sports, clubs, boarding, and a community where every learner belongs.

Sports Clubs & Societies Boarding Community
Sports

Building healthy bodies and teamwork

Our learners take part in a wide range of sports — from football and netball to athletics and volleyball.

Football

Our boys' and girls' teams train weekly and compete in district leagues.

Netball

A favourite among our girls — fast, fun, and full of team spirit.

Athletics

Track and field events prepare learners for inter-school competitions.

Volleyball

Both boys and girls train together, building coordination and teamwork.

Clubs & Societies

Where interests grow into passions

From music to debate, our clubs give every learner a place to explore what they love.

Music Club

Learners sing, play instruments, and perform at school events.

Debate Club

Sharpens public speaking and critical thinking through friendly competitions.

Science Club

Hands-on experiments and science fairs that bring learning to life.

Drama Club

Learners write, rehearse, and perform plays for the whole school.

Boarding

A home away from home

Our boarding facilities offer a safe, supportive environment where learners live, study, and grow together.

Comfortable dormitories

Clean, spacious rooms with supervision and a focus on safety.

Evening study

Structured study time with teacher support every evening.

Dining hall

Balanced meals are prepared fresh daily in our school kitchen.

Community

A place where everyone belongs

Our learners come from across Luwero District and beyond — and together they form a community that feels like family.

School assemblies

Weekly gatherings to celebrate achievements and share news.

Community service

Learners take part in outreach projects that give back to Luwero.

Celebrations

Cultural days, sports days, and end-of-term events bring us together.
