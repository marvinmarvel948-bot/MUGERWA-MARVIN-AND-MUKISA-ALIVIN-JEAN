Common questions

Where is Bright High School Luwero located?

The school is listed at Plot 14, Kampala–Gulu Road, Luwero District, Uganda. Contact the school before travelling if you need directions.

Which levels of education does the school offer?

The website describes Elementary School (Primary 1–7) and High School (Senior 1–6) programmes. Contact the school to confirm current classes and available places.

How can I apply for admission?

Visit the Admissions page for the application steps and entry requirements, or contact the school office for the current application form.

How can parents contact the school?

Call +256 755 630074 or email info@brighthighluwero.ac.ug.

Does the school provide boarding?

The school website describes boarding information on the Student Life page. Contact the school to confirm current boarding availability, requirements and fees.
