Academics

A rigorous, learner-centred curriculum from Elementary through High School — preparing every student for life.

Elementary School High School Our Approach Results
Elementary School

A strong foundation for lifelong learning

Our Elementary programme (Primary 1–7) builds literacy, numeracy, and curiosity in a warm, supportive environment.

Literacy

Daily reading, writing, and speaking practice — with a strong phonics foundation in the early years.

Numeracy

Hands-on mathematics that builds confidence and problem-solving skills from an early age.

Science & Discovery

Simple experiments and outdoor learning that spark curiosity about the natural world.

Social Studies

Ugandan and East African history, geography, and citizenship.

Creative Arts

Music, art, and drama — because creativity matters as much as academics.

Physical Education

Daily play and sport that build healthy bodies and teamwork.

High School

Preparing students for UCE, UACE, and beyond

Our High School programme (S1–S6) combines rigorous academics with strong pastoral care and career guidance.

Sciences

Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Mathematics taught in fully equipped laboratories.

Humanities

History, Geography, Literature, and Religious Education — developing critical thinkers.

Business & Commerce

Accounting, Commerce, and Economics for future entrepreneurs and professionals.

ICT

Practical computer skills and digital literacy for the modern world.

Languages

English, Luganda, and Kiswahili — strong communication in a multilingual world.

Career Guidance

One-on-one support to help students choose subjects and pathways that fit their goals.

Our Approach

How we teach

Small classes, dedicated teachers, and a focus on the whole child.

Small classes

Average 18–24 learners per class — so every child is known and supported.

Qualified teachers

All our teachers are trained, registered, and committed to ongoing professional development.

Regular assessment

Termly reports and parent-teacher meetings keep families informed and involved.

Holistic development

Academics, character, creativity, and physical wellbeing — all part of the same education.

Results

Academic performance

A track record of strong results at every level.

98% UCE pass rate

Consistently among the top-performing schools in Luwero District.

Top PLE performers

Our Elementary learners regularly earn places at leading secondary schools.

University placements

Our High School graduates go on to study at Makerere, Kyambogo, and beyond.
