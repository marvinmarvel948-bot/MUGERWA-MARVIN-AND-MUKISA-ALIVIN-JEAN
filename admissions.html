Simple Process

How to Apply in 6 Steps

From first inquiry to first day of class — here is exactly what to expect.

1. Obtain the form

Collect an application form from the school office or download it online.

2. Complete & submit

Attach all required documents and submit in person or via email.

3. Pay application fee

UGX 30,000 (non-refundable) via bank deposit or mobile money. Keep the receipt.

4. Placement assessment

Elementary: literacy & numeracy. High School: English, Math, Science.

5. Receive admission letter

Successful applicants are notified within 3 working days.

6. Confirm your place

Pay the deposit by the deadline stated in your letter and secure your spot.

Entry Requirements

Bring originals plus one set of photocopies on submission day.

Elementary School

  • Completed previous grade report card
  • Copy of birth certificate
  • Two passport-size photographs
  • Parent / guardian ID copy
  • Completed application form
  • Application fee receipt

High School

  • PLE results slip (S1 entry) or equivalent
  • School leaving certificate / transfer letter
  • Copies of previous termly reports
  • Two passport-size photographs
  • Parent / guardian ID copy
  • Application fee receipt

All Applicants

  • Completed and signed application form
  • Birth certificate copy
  • Previous school reports / results slip
  • Transfer letter (where applicable)
  • Two passport-size photographs
  • Parent / guardian ID copy
  • Proof of payment (application fee)
  • Medical/immunization record (Elementary)

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there an entrance exam?

Yes — a short placement assessment. For Elementary, it covers literacy and numeracy and is not pass/fail. For High School it covers English, Mathematics, and Science.

Can my child join mid-term?

Yes, subject to space availability and approval by the Head Teacher.

Do you offer scholarships or bursaries?

Limited need-based bursaries are available each year, plus merit awards for top PLE performers. Applications close 30 November.

What is the class size?

Average 18–24 learners per class, with a maximum of 30.

Are payment plans available?

Yes. Termly fees can be split into two instalments by arrangement with the Bursar.

Is boarding available?

Boarding is available for High School learners. Spaces are limited and allocated on a first-come basis.
