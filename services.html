What We Offer

Three learning paths

Every learner at Bright High School Luwero follows one of three paths — each designed to build knowledge, character, and confidence.

Elementary School (P1–P7)

A strong, warm foundation in literacy, numeracy, science, and social studies — plus creative arts and physical education every week.

High School (S1–S6)

Rigorous O-Level and A-Level study in the sciences, humanities, business, ICT, and languages, with pastoral care and career guidance.

Co-curricular Programmes

Music, debate, science club, and sports — because education goes beyond the classroom and builds the whole child.

Guidance

Choosing the right path

Not sure which programme fits? Here is a short guide to help learners and families decide where to start.

Starting at Elementary

Best for learners aged 6–12 who are beginning formal education, or transferring from another primary school. Covers P1 to P7 and leads to the PLE.

Entering High School

For learners who have completed P7 and wish to join S1, or who are transferring in with a valid results slip and a school leaving letter.

Joining a co-curricular

Open to all enrolled learners — pick one or more clubs at the start of each term. No additional fee is required.
