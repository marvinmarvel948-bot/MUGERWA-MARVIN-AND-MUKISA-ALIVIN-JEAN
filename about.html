Our Story

From a small classroom to a thriving school

Bright High School Luwero opened its doors in 2003 with just one classroom and 24 learners. Two decades later, we are a full Elementary and High School serving hundreds of students from across Luwero District and beyond.

2003

Founded with one classroom and a simple promise: to give every child a quality education close to home.

2010

Expanded to High School, adding science labs, a library, and boarding facilities.

2018

Record UCE results — 98% pass rate, placing us among the top schools in the district.

Today

Over 600 learners, 40+ staff, and a thriving alumni community giving back to Luwero.

Our Mission

To provide a holistic, learner-centred education that equips every student with the knowledge, skills, and values to succeed in life and contribute positively to their community.

Our Vision

To be the leading school in Luwero District — a place where academic excellence meets character formation, and every learner is prepared for the world beyond the classroom.

What We Stand For

Core Values

These values guide everything we do — from the classroom to the playing field.

Excellence

We set high expectations and support every learner to reach their full potential.

Respect

Integrity

Community

We are rooted in Luwero and committed to serving our community.

Curiosity

Resilience
